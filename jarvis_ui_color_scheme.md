# JARVIS UI Futuristic Color Scheme

## Primary Colors
- **DEEP_SPACE_BG**: #0A0E17 - Dark space background
- **NEON_BLUE**: #00A6FF - Bright holographic blue
- **ACCENT_BLUE**: #0072BB - Secondary blue
- **NEON_CYAN**: #00FFFF - Bright cyan for highlights
- **GRAPHITE_GRAY**: #303540 - Dark panel background
- **CHROME**: #C0C0C0 - Metallic accents
- **TEXT_COLOR**: #E0F0FF - Soft blue-white text

## Accent Colors
- **NEON_GREEN**: #39FF14 - Success states
- **NEON_RED**: #FF003C - Active/recording states
- **NEON_ORANGE**: #FF7F00 - Warning states
- **NEON_PURPLE**: #9D00FF - Secondary accent

## Metallic Gradient
- **METAL_DARK**: #1A1A2E - Dark metallic
- **METAL_MEDIUM**: #303050 - Medium metallic
- **METAL_LIGHT**: #5E5E8E - Light metallic highlight
